# Customer Research & Insights

## Target Customer Segments

**1. Avid Readers (35% of target market)**

- Age: 25-55
- Income: $50K-$120K
- Reads 20+ books/year
- Values: Discovery, community, quality curation

**2. Students & Academics (30% of target market)**

- Age: 18-35
- Income: $15K-$60K
- Needs: Textbooks, research materials, affordability
- Values: Price, availability, digital access

**3. Professional Development (20% of target market)**

- Age: 28-50
- Income: $75K-$200K
- Needs: Business books, self-improvement, audiobooks
- Values: Convenience, recommendations, time efficiency

**4. Gift Buyers (15% of target market)**

- Age: 30-65
- Income: $40K-$150K
- Needs: Curated gift options, subscriptions
- Values: Presentation, personalization, reliability

## Customer Feedback Analysis

**Survey Results (1,200 respondents, Q4 2025):**

**Pain Points with Current Platforms:**

- 68% - "Too many irrelevant recommendations"
- 54% - "Difficult to discover new authors"
- 47% - "Checkout process too complicated"
- 42% - "Poor mobile experience"
- 38% - "Limited community features"
- 35% - "Expensive shipping costs"

**Most Desired Features:**

- 72% - Personalized book recommendations based on reading history
- 65% - Virtual book club integration
- 58% - Ability to preview first chapter before purchase
- 51% - Reading progress tracking across devices
- 48% - Author Q&A and virtual events
- 44% - Subscription box for curated monthly selections

**Customer Testimonials (Beta Testing Group):**

> "The book club feature connected me with readers who share my interests. It's like a social reading platform meets e-commerce, but better." - James T., Professional

> "As a student, I love that I can find both new and used textbooks in one place with price comparisons." - Emily R., Graduate Student

> "The one-click audiobook-to-ebook sync is a game changer for my commute." - Michael K., Business Executive
