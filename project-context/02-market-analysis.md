# Market Analysis

## Market Overview

- **Global Online Book Market Size**: $26.4 billion (2025)
- **Projected Growth Rate**: 5.8% CAGR through 2030
- **Target Market Share Goal**: 2.5% within 3 years ($660M annual revenue)
